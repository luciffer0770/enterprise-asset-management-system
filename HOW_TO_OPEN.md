# Open the App (No Terminal Needed)

Tool and trolley management with tickets, KPIs, Excel import/export. The app is already running. Use these steps:

---

## Option 1: Ports Panel (click only)

1. Look at the **bottom panel** of Cursor.
2. Click the **Ports** tab.
3. Find **port 3000** in the list.
4. Click the **globe icon** (or "Open in Browser") next to port 3000.

---

## Option 2: Simple Browser (keyboard shortcut)

1. Press **Ctrl+Shift+P** (Mac: **Cmd+Shift+P**).
2. Type: **Simple Browser**
3. Click **Simple Browser: Show**.
4. In the box, type: **http://localhost:3000**
5. Press Enter.

---
